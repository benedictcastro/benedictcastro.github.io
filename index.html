Excel workflow automation / Billing

Sales Invoice Generator

A structured Excel/VBA workflow that turns billing inputs into consistently formatted sales invoices and PDF files.

Excel 2019VBA / MacrosPDF workflow
Enter billing details/Generate invoice/Save PDF & summary

The challenge

Preparing invoices involves repeated entry of customer details, invoice numbers, descriptions, and amounts. Each invoice also needs the same layout for printing and an identifiable PDF filename.

What I built

  • A generator that places registered name, address, TIN, branch, description, date, and amount into a predefined invoice template.
  • Invoice numbering based on existing invoice sheets, with a separate sheet for each sales invoice.
  • Template-based generation that preserves the invoice’s formatting and standardizes mapped text.
  • PDF export using the invoice number and branch or registered customer name in the filename.
  • An SI Summary that consolidates invoice records and appends new invoice numbers without duplicating existing entries.
  • Invoice lookup by SI number for retrieving previously generated details.

Practical outcome

Supports approximately 900 invoices per month. Estimated processing time decreased from 10–11 minutes to about 5 minutes per invoice, including preparation, PDF saving, printing, and scanning. This represents approximately 5–6 minutes saved per invoice, or 75–90 hours monthly at that volume. These are estimates based on my typical workflow; customer delivery time is excluded.

This case study describes the Excel/VBA workflow. Internal invoice files and customer information are not published.

Moneyhub Inc. / Leasing & lending

AR Monitoring Automation

Improvements to existing receivables workbooks that connect customer records, collection monitoring, and summary reporting.

Excel formulasVBA / MacrosPivotTablesAI-assisted tools
Customer & tenant records/Connected summaries/AR aging reports

The challenge

Manual encoding and repeated preparation of receivables reports made collection recording and AR reporting time-consuming. Information needed to be easier to maintain across leasing and lending records.

My contribution

  • Enhanced existing monitoring workbooks through automation, dashboards, and summary reports.
  • Created a one-click process for setting up customer and tenant information sheets.
  • Used Excel formulas to automatically update summary reports from underlying records.
  • Developed a one-click AR aging report generator using VBA/macros.
  • Organized receivables information to support collection tracking and clearer account review.

Practical outcome

Faster collection recording and AR reporting, fewer manual mistakes, improved accuracy, and clearer visibility of receivables information.

This was an improvement to existing internal workbooks. Confidential records and internal workbook files are not published.

Receivables reporting / Data visualization

AR Visual Dashboard

Excel dashboards and summary reports that bring detailed receivables records into a clearer view for review and discussion.

ExcelPivotTablesExcel formulasData visualization
Receivables records/Summaries & PivotTables/Visual overview

The challenge

Detailed customer and tenant records are necessary for accounting, but long worksheets can make the overall receivables position harder to understand.

My contribution

  • Created dashboards and summary reports as part of the receivables monitoring improvements.
  • Used Excel formulas and PivotTables to organize information from underlying records.
  • Presented receivables data in a clearer format to support review, reporting, and collection discussions.

Practical outcome

Previously, reviewing receivables involved manual filtering and repeated SUM/SUMIFS calculations in numerical reports. After data import, the dashboard updates automatically. One-click category filters and visual reports make sales, collections, outstanding balances, and overdue accounts easier for management to review.

No internal dashboard screenshot or client dataset is published. The project card illustrates the reporting workflow.
